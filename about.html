About RevMotion

Cars, boats, and straight information — from Orlando.

RevMotion Auto & Marine LLC is a Florida company based in Orlando. It publishes car, boat, and market news and buying education, and it runs a buyer's-agent procurement service that helps people buy cars the careful way: searched, inspected, reported, and purchased in the buyer's own name.

See How It Works

What RevMotion does today

Two things, done in the open. First, information: a news page covering cars and boats — Central Florida, the wider world, and the new models coming — plus a market lane that follows the stock market in plain language, and a learning center that teaches the unglamorous parts of buying: titles, liens, VIN and HIN numbers, inspections, and paperwork.

Second, buyer procurement. A buyer tells RevMotion the vehicle they want and their all-in budget. RevMotion searches public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers, inspects the serious candidates, and reports back with photos and facts. The buyer then purchases directly from the seller, in the buyer's own name. RevMotion never owns the vehicle and never holds the purchase money. RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins.

How RevMotion works — and what it will not do

For the buyer only

RevMotion works for the buyer, on the buyer's instructions and budget. It does not represent sellers, does not set sellers' prices, and takes no money from sellers.

The agreement in writing, first

Every engagement starts with a signed procurement agreement that sets out the terms and the scope of the search before any work begins.

Reports, not promises

Inspection reports are honest observations — photos, condition notes, and title status as represented — not warranties. The buyer always makes the final call.

Where RevMotion stands today

RevMotion was formed in Florida in 2025 and is building toward a full licensed operation: the dealer license, bond, insurance, and premises that Florida requires are being assembled in that order. Until the license is in hand, the company does the work the law allows without one — news, education, and buyer procurement — and says so plainly.

RevMotion Auto & Marine LLC is not a motor vehicle dealership and does not sell vehicles. Dealer and seller services are in preparation while the company's Florida dealer licensing is completed; seller intake is on hold until then. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell a vehicle.
