Buying Costs

Where the money goes when you buy through RevMotion.

When you buy through RevMotion, you buy the vehicle from its seller, in your own name — RevMotion never owns it and never holds your purchase money. This page walks through the costs of a purchase in plain terms, so nothing about the money is a surprise.

The vehicle's price

The biggest cost of any purchase is the vehicle itself. That price is agreed between you and the seller, and you pay the seller directly — RevMotion never holds purchase money and never appears on the title.

Paid to the seller

The vehicle's price, agreed between you and the seller. You pay the seller directly; the title passes from the seller to you.

Paid to the state

Florida sales tax, the title transfer, and registration are the buyer's own costs, handled at the tax collector when the vehicle is titled and registered in your name.

Your own arrangements

Insurance is yours to arrange, and so is transport if you choose to have the vehicle delivered instead of collecting it yourself.

RevMotion's service

RevMotion's work is the search and the checking, done before you spend anything on a vehicle:

  • The search across public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers.
  • Inspection of the serious candidates, in person where they stand.
  • Written reports with photos, condition notes, and title status as represented.
  • Coordination of the purchase steps, so the buyer buys directly from the seller, in the buyer's own name.

RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins.

Clear terms: RevMotion's fee is for procurement services. It is not a car price, not a deposit toward a car, and not a down payment.
