Procurement Fees

One fee. In writing. Paid only when the car is yours.

RevMotion Procurement charges a single fee for the search, inspection, and reporting work — agreed in a signed agreement before any work starts. There are no hourly charges, no retainer, and no fee at all if no vehicle is delivered.

The fee

The procurement fee is 10% of the vehicle's purchase price, with a minimum of $600 — whichever is greater. It is paid at delivery, when the buyer has the vehicle.

A $4,000 car

10% would be $400 — below the minimum, so the fee is $600.

An $8,000 car

10% of the purchase price — the fee is $800.

A $20,000 car

10% of the purchase price — the fee is $2,000.

What the fee covers

  • The search across public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers.
  • Inspection of the serious candidates, in person where they stand.
  • Written reports with photos, condition notes, and title status as represented.
  • Coordination of the purchase steps, so the buyer buys directly from the seller, in the buyer's own name.

What the fee does not cover

  • The price of the vehicle itself — paid by the buyer directly to the seller. RevMotion never holds purchase money.
  • Florida tax, title, and registration costs that belong to the purchase.
  • Transport or delivery, if the buyer arranges it separately.
Clear terms: the fee is for procurement services. It is not a car price, not a deposit toward a car, and not a down payment. It is named in the signed procurement agreement before the search starts — never a surprise at the end.
If the search comes up empty: the fee is $0. RevMotion would rather report an honest empty search than push a bad car to earn a fee.
