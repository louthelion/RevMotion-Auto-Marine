Buyer Procurement

Tell us the car you are hunting. We find it — you buy it in your name.

RevMotion Procurement is a buyer's-agent service for cars, trucks, motorcycles, RVs, and trailers. You tell us the vehicle you want and the all-in budget you can work with. We search public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers, inspect the serious candidates, and report back with photos and facts — the good and the bad.

When you pick one, you buy it directly from the seller, in your own name. RevMotion never owns the vehicle, never holds your purchase money, and never appears on the title. RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins.

How It Works

From request to your name on the title — five steps.

01

Send the buyer request

Tell us the vehicle, the must-haves, your all-in budget, and when you need it. Exact requests get exact searches; vague requests get honest questions first.

02

The fit call

We talk it through before anything is signed. If the budget cannot buy the vehicle in today's market, we say so plainly instead of taking on a search that cannot succeed.

03

Sign the procurement agreement

The agreement sets out the terms, what the search covers, and how long it runs. Nothing starts before the agreement is signed.

04

The search and the reports

We work public auctions, government sales, and private sellers, and inspect the serious candidates. You get photos, condition notes, and title status as the seller represents it — reports are honest observations, not warranties.

05

You buy it — in your name

You pay the seller directly and the title passes from the seller to you.

Buying in Central Florida

RevMotion is based in Orlando and works the Central Florida market: Orange, Seminole, Osceola, and Lake counties, and the auction and private-sale lanes that feed them. Florida titles, lien payoffs, and registration are handled as part of buying in your own name, and every step is verified before money moves.

One honest note on where RevMotion stands: RevMotion is not a car dealership and does not sell cars. Seller intake is on hold while the company's dealer licensing is completed. What runs today is what you see on this page — car, boat, and market news, buying education, and buyer procurement.

Does RevMotion sell cars?

No. RevMotion has no lot, no inventory, and no cars for sale. We work for buyers: we find the vehicle, you buy it from the seller in your name.

What does the service cost?

RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins.

Can you get a car from a dealer-only auction?

No — dealer-only lanes require a dealer license, and we will not pretend otherwise. Our search runs through public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers.

RevMotion Auto & Marine LLC provides news, education, and buyer procurement services. RevMotion is not a motor vehicle dealer and does not sell vehicles.
