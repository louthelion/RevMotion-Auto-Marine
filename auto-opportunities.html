Auto — Buying

Cars, motorcycles, RVs, and trailers — bought the careful way.

RevMotion does not keep a lot or a listings board. Buyers come to us with a request, and the search runs where the vehicles actually are: public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers.

Start a Buyer Request

What buyers ask us to find

Cars & Trucks

Daily drivers, family vehicles, work trucks, and first cars. A sharp request names the year range, the mileage ceiling, the must-have features, and the all-in budget — with tax and title thought through from day one, not discovered at the end.

Request a Car

Motorcycles

Cruisers, sport bikes, and dual-sports. Engine size, mileage, title status, and service history matter more than shiny photos — our inspection reports cover all four, plus tires, chain or belt, and any drop damage the seller forgot to mention.

Request a Motorcycle

RVs & Trailers

Travel trailers, campers, utility and enclosed trailers. Roof condition and water damage, tire age, brakes, and title paperwork are where RV money is lost — the inspection report goes there first, before anyone falls in love with the inside.

Request an RV / Trailer

Where the search looks

Public auctions

Open auction lanes where buyers — or their representative — can bid without a dealer license. This is where fleet, bank, and everyday vehicles change hands every week in Central Florida.

Government surplus sales

City, county, and state vehicles and equipment sold as fleets rotate. The paperwork is clean and the condition varies — which is exactly why the inspection report matters.

Private sellers

The classifieds-and-driveways market. Every candidate is verified in person — VIN, title, and condition — before it is ever reported to a buyer as a serious option.

What we cannot do: bid in dealer-only auction lanes, or sell you a car ourselves. RevMotion is a buyer's agent — the purchase is always between you and the seller, in your name. RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins.
