How It Works

A buyer's agent for your next car — step by step.

RevMotion Procurement helps you buy a car without doing the hunting yourself — and without RevMotion ever becoming the seller. Here is exactly how an engagement runs, from the first request to your name on the title.

The procurement process

01

Tell us the car

Send the buyer request: the vehicle, the year range, the must-haves — color, features, mileage ceiling — your all-in budget, and your timeline. The more exact the request, the sharper the search.

02

The fit call

A short, honest conversation before anything is signed. Can this budget buy this car in this market? If it cannot, we say so on the call — no agreement is signed on a fantasy search.

03

Sign the procurement agreement

The agreement sets out the terms, what the search covers, and the search window. Only after it is signed does the search start.

04

We search

Public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers across Central Florida and beyond. Every serious candidate is inspected in person where it stands.

05

You get the reports

Photos, condition notes, and title status as the seller represents it, for each serious candidate. Reports are observations, not warranties — and the decision is always yours.

06

You buy it — in your name

You pay the seller directly, and the title passes from the seller to you.

What RevMotion never does: take title to a vehicle, hold a buyer's purchase money, list or advertise a seller's car, or represent a seller. Those lines are what keep this service lawful without a dealer license — and they protect the buyer, too.
