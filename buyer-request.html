How Procurement Works

Before you submit — know how this works.

This form starts a buyer's-agent search. Tell us the vehicle you want — year, make, model, color, must-haves — your all-in budget, and your timeline. The more exact the request, the sharper the search we can run.

After you submit, we schedule a short fit call. If the budget can buy the vehicle in today's market, we sign a procurement agreement and start the search across public auctions, government sales, and private sellers. You receive inspection reports with photos and facts, you choose, and you buy directly from the seller in your own name.

RevMotion is paid a fee for a completed procurement, set out in a signed agreement before the search begins. RevMotion never takes title and never holds your purchase money.

What happens after you submit

01

We read it and call you

Every request gets a fit call. We confirm the vehicle, the budget, and the timeline — and if the numbers cannot work, we tell you on that call, not after weeks of silence.

02

The agreement

If the search makes sense, the procurement agreement is signed first: fee, scope, and search window in writing, before any work begins.

03

The search

Public auctions, government surplus sales, and private sellers. Serious candidates are inspected and reported with photos, condition notes, and title status as represented.

04

Your decision

Reports are observations, not warranties. You decide which vehicle — if any — is right, and you can walk away from any candidate.

05

Purchase and delivery

You pay the seller directly; the title passes from the seller to you.

Do I pay anything to submit?

No. Submitting a request costs nothing.

I want a boat — can you procure one?

The procurement service covers motor vehicles: cars, trucks, motorcycles, RVs, and trailers. Boat news and buying education are on this site either way — and if you send a boat request, we will tell you plainly what we can and cannot do for it today.

How long does a search take?

It depends on how rare the vehicle is and how firm the budget is. The agreement names the search window before work starts, and we report what the market is actually doing while we search.
