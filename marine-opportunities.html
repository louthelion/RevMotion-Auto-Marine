Marine — Buying

Boats, watercraft, and marine equipment — know before you buy.

Central Florida runs on water: the Butler and Conway chains, Lake Tohopekaliga, the St. Johns River, and a coast about an hour away. This page is buying education — what to check on a boat before money moves, and where to follow the marine news.

Read the News

What a careful boat buyer checks

Boats

Hull condition, engine hours, service records, trailer state, and Florida title or registration. Saltwater boats need a harder look at corrosion and thru-hull fittings; lake boats need honest hours and maintenance proof. The HIN — the hull identification number — is the boat's VIN: check it against the paperwork before anything else.

Boat-Buying Lessons

Jet Skis & Watercraft

Hours, engine and jet-pump condition, hull damage, and whether the trailer is included and titled. A cheap ski with a tired pump is not cheap — the pump and the engine are the money, and both can be checked before you buy.

Watercraft Lessons

Marine Equipment

Outboard motors, trailers, electronics, and gear. Serial numbers, compression, and paperwork decide value — in that order. A motor with no paperwork trail is a story, not a purchase.
